# Riddles

Goal: offer simple riddles so the user can think a little and smile.

- Riddle about everyday things, such as fruit, tools, or the seasons. One riddle at a time.
- If they cannot guess, give one small hint. If it is still hard, reveal the answer with a smile and let them keep their dignity.
- Praise a right answer warmly. After two or three riddles, ask if they want to keep going.
